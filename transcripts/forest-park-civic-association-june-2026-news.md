In response to concerns about personal safety while making a sale or purchase utilizing an online
auction or person to person exchange, the City of Columbus, Department of Public Safety has
created five (5) monitored Internet Purchase Exchange Zones around the City. These zones will be
located at Columbus Division of Police (CPD) Sub-stations.
Residents will be able to conduct their transaction at any of the five locations knowing that the
exchange is being recorded with a City-owned video surveillance camera. In addition, since the
locations involve a police sub-station, marked vehicles could be expected to be on location at any
time.

Station #1 - 8118 Sancus Blvd. Westerville, OH 43081 (Far North)

Station #5 - 1371 Cleveland Ave. Columbus, OH 43211 (Northeast)
Station #10 - 4215 Clime Rd. Columbus, OH 43228 (Far West)
Station #20 - 2500 Park Crescent Dr. Columbus, OH 43232 (Far East)

camera. Users are encouraged to conduct their transaction near the sign for

Forest Park Civic Association Membership Update
Summer has arrived in Forest Park, and with it comes graduation parties, vacations, cookouts, and all the activity that
makes this neighborhood feel alive. As many of us settle into the season, I wanted to provide a quick update on the
Forest Park Civic Association membership drive and ask for your continued support.
First, thank you to the many residents who have already renewed or joined FPCA this year. We sincerely appreciate it.
Strong early participation showed once again that Forest Park residents care deeply about this neighborhood and the
future of our community.
As we move into June, however, we are still behind where we need to be in overall memberships. Many long-time
members have not yet renewed. In my experience, that usually is not a statement about the organization — life simply
gets busy, the form gets set aside, and weeks pass quickly. If that sounds familiar, there is still plenty of time to renew.
Your membership matters more than ever. Forest Park continues to face the same challenges and opportunities that
come with being a large, established Columbus neighborhood. Development activity around the 161 corridor will contin-
ue to shape the future of this area, and having a strong, organized neighborhood voice is essential. The larger and
more engaged our membership is, the stronger our ability to advocate for Forest Park residents.
FPCA continues to support the neighborhood in many ways:
 Funding supplemental security patrols
 Working with Columbus Police and city officials
 Supporting block watch efforts
 Advocating on zoning and development issues
 Sponsoring neighborhood events and activities
 Maintaining communication through the Forester, website, and social media
 Helping preserve the overall quality and reputation of Forest Park
None of this happens without resident support.
Membership dues are currently $30 per household annually. While costs have increased over the years, the mission
remains the same: protecting and strengthening the neighborhood we all share.
I also want to mention that earlier in the membership drive, some residents may have experienced difficulties using
PayPal through the FPCA website. Fortunately, those issues have been resolved, and online payments are now func-
tioning properly. Joining online is once again a fast and convenient option.
You can renew or join online at:
https://www.fpcivic.org/join/
If you prefer, you may also mail in your membership form. And if transportation or scheduling is a challenge, arrange-
ments can even be made for membership pickup.
Forest Park has always been strongest when neighbors support one another and invest in the community around them.
Thank you to everyone who continues to do exactly that.
With appreciation,
Mike Stone, Membership Coordinator
