rather than behind it.
Though the Committee had concerns about the elimination of all required EV charging spaces from the site, it
noted that the number of parking spaces on the site overall barely exceeded the threshold below which the
requirement would automatically be waived under §3312.57, and that the vast majority of the spaces on the site as a
whole are in fact not employee or customer parking per se but used to stage vehicles for repair. Consequently, it
agreed that the practical and financial hardship of installing EV capable and/or EV ready spaces was greater than the
potential benefit to customers or the community.
