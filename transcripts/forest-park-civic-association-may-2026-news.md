Submitted by: Outreach Committee Chair – Michael Cote – michaelcote@fpcivic.org
The North YMCA hosted the Third Annual One Columbus Culture Day on May 1, 2026. Many residents in Forest Park celebrated a fun-filled evening of dance, music, and international food sponsored by the City of Columbus, Department of Neighborhoods, New American Program. The New American Program is an initiative designed to help immigrants and refugees integrate into the community by fostering economic, civic, and cultural engagement. Our celebration of diverse cultures is a hallmark of what makes Columbus such a vibrant and welcoming city.
Northland High School held its Second Annual Culture Festival, May 8, 2026. Many residents of Forest Park joined students, parents, faculty, administrators, and staff to enjoy a memorable evening of dance, musical performances, cooking demonstrations, and a delicious international meal! The students of NHS demonstrated a level of talent, hospitality, and enthusiasm that exemplifies the richness and diversity of cultures that makes Northland such a wonderful place to live. Northland High School makes Northland proud to be the international gateway to Columbus. Go Vikings!
Northland High School held its inaugural Northland Market, May 9, 2026. Over 80 vendors, crafters, organizations, and community groups were registered, including the FPCA. This was a great opportunity for  our civic association to network with organizations, meet new Forest Park residents, and we actually signed up new members! The Northland Market was a Community Campus School initiative to promote community networking and community involvement in Columbus City Schools. NHS deserves a resounding round of applause for organizing and hosting one the best community events ever held in Northland! With such an auspicious start we hope NHS will continue to host the Northland Market as an annual event for years to come. Go Vikings!

April 2026 Outreach Report
Submitted by Michael Cote
Friends of Woodward Park will be kicking off Earth Day 2026 with a community event at Woodward Park
and Nature Preserve on April 25, Saturday 2:30 - 4:30. Keep Columbus Beautiful will be providing litter
pickup tools, safety vests, buckets and trash bags for about twenty volunteers to use during the Wood-
ward Park Cleanup event. The Friends of Woodward Park is a newly formed group of volunteers from
the Forest Park and Karmel - Woodward Park neighborhoods who are working with the Columbus Rec-
reation and Parks Department to enhance and sustain the beauty and conservation of Woodward Park
and Nature Preserve. Anyone interested in learning about the Friends of Woodward Park and helping
with the Cleanup event on April 25 please contact:
Joanne Salyer, Karmel - Woodward Park Civic Association, Karmelwoodwardparkbw@outlook.com
and/ or Michael Cote, Forest Park Civic Association, michaelcote@fpcivic.org
The North YMCA will be hosting The One Columbus Culture Day sponsored by the
Columbus Department of Neighborhood’s The New American Program on Friday,
May 1, 4:30 - 7:30. This free event will offer international food, music, live perfor-
mances, and valuable community resources.
Northland High School will be hosting its Inaugural Northland Market on Saturday
May 9, 2026 10 am - 1:00 pm. The faculty and students of NHS have organized a fun-filled community
event with music, games, local vendors, and food trucks. A table and chairs have been reserved for the
Forest Park Civic Association. This is a prime opportunity to meet members and friends of the NHS
community and introduce them to what our civic association does for the Forest Park community.
We’ll need volunteers to dress up our booth and table with the FPCA banner and welcome flags. We’ll
also need copies of the FPCA “brag sheet” and membership forms to distribute and sign up new mem-
bers.
Katie Van Mercetta - FPCA’s 2026 Northland Community Council Volunteer Recipient
Katie Van Mercetta is an integral part of the Forest Park community and the Forest Park Civic
Association (FPCA). She has been instrumental in helping set up Google Workspace for
nonprofits; thus, helping the FPCA remain relevant in the digital age.
She was the driving force to reestablish our membership database and continues to keep its
data updated. Membership database upkeep is essential to maintaining membership data,
making sure the FPCA goals and initiatives reflect our community and membership needs/
demographics, and to meeting our membership drive goals, which is vital to our ability to
continue to serve our community.
Katie (along with her husband Daryl) are their Area Representatives and have served as Supplemental Security
Officers for many years. Within her role as the Supplemental Security Officer, Katie writes the monthly
supplemental security column in The Forester.
In addition to her numerous roles within the Forest Park Civic Association, she continues to volunteer for critical
FPCA activities from National Night Out, to helping at the 4th of July parade, to sitting at the membership table at
various events. She (along with Daryl) have helped with mulch delivery (FPCA fundraiser) over the last several
years.
It is through Katie’s dedication to the Forest Park community and FPCA that essential components of FPCA
continue to function year after year. We are proud to recognize Katie’s hard work and contribution to our
community via essential and often laborious work.

Hello Forest Park!
We were lucky to have Officer Hicks join us at our April meeting and he
went over stats for the precinct he oversees, Precinct 18, which is not
exclusive to but includes Forest Park. There were a total of 898 incidents
for the whole precinct, not just our neighborhood, and a majority of them
were domestic violence calls followed by car thefts. He urged people to
remember to keep their car doors locked, don't leave your cars running,
and don't leave valuables in your car. Surprisingly, he said that most crimes occur between 3pm - 11pm
on Wednesdays and not in the middle of the night or on weekends as most people assume. If you want
a more detailed description of the crimes on your street, you can do so at communitycrimemap.com.
There has also been an update to the law regarding stealing packages from porches
aka "porch pirating." It is now considered a felony, which carries heftier
consequences than a misdemeanor. You may have seen signs placed at our
entrances informing people of the same over the holidays. Many of the signs are in
the process of being replaced and if you would like a sign for your front yard, please
reach out to security@fpcivic.org and we'll see if we can accommodate your request.
Likewise, if you have any questions or concerns that you'd like to pass on to Officer
Hicks, you can do so by reaching out to the same email address.
The membership drive is upon us and one of the benefits of being a member is Supplemental Security.
The membership is only $30 per year for the entire household and is essential to keeping our civic
association alive. If you're traveling or otherwise away from your home for a period of time, you can
notify Daryl Van Mercetta via email at security@fpcivic.org, via mail at 2131 Honeytree Court,
Columbus, OH 43229, or by phone at 513-479-2416. He will submit your information to our security
company who will then conduct additional patrols in your absence.
As always, if you see something, say something and stay safe everyone.
Michael Cote - FPCA’s 2026 Northland Community Council Volunteer Recipient
Michael Cote is incredibly involved in Forest Park and the greater Northland Community. He has brought a
passion for community, human connection, and civic engagement to the Forest Park Civic Association (FPCA).
It is through his leadership and natural collaborative nature that the FPCA was able to hold the
FPCA New American’s Party in June of 2025, which was part of the Civic Integration Seed
Grant through The City of Columbus: Department of Neighborhoods. Some highlights of
Michael’s contribution to this event include acquiring the venue, securing speakers and
guests from local and city government departments and nonprofit organizations, 2 chefs for
catering, and a soundengineer/DJ.
Michael currently serves as the FPCA’s Outreach Chair, which was a position created specifi-
cally for Michael due to the vital role he serves within our community. His role as the Outreach Chair is
exemplified by his engagement and involvement with many community leaders and organizations–including
Gille Community Center, Northland High School Community Campus School, and multiple local and city
government leaders. We are proud and honored to have Michael as part of the Forest Park Civic Association and
as a neighbor in the Forest Park Community.
