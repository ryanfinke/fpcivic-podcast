NCC March MeetingUpdate
Submitted by Ken Gilbert
1, We have a new Columbus Police liaison in the 18th Precinct. Officer Yandrich has moved to the mounted horse
patrol. Our new liaison is Officer Duane Hicks. He has the same phone number (614)-645 1418 and his email is
Dhicks@columbuspolice.org
2. Armed robberies are up—often connected with Facebook Marketplace deals. Be careful! No Parking signs have been
posted around the area to deal with the semi truck issue. City Attorney Klein will be holding a Block by Block
community safety meeting March 11 at 6 pm at Northern Lights Library.
3. We have a new Zone Attorney from the City Attorney’s office, Heidi Carr, who was a former Zone 5 attorney and
also worked with the US Attorney’s Office. We’ve been asked to submit issues to 311 first and then contact the City
Attorney’s Office with that info. A 311 report gives them a good place to start.
4. NABA (Northland Area Business Association) notes:
 They reported that there would be no quarterly luncheon.
 NABA needs more volunteers for the 161 Clean Up days.
 Devin Deal, our city liaison reports on a number of items.
 Check the city website for summer internships for teens.
 A Town Hall took place on March 4 about a new mapping project.
 There were public workshops on March 19 at Bethel UMC. Rise Up Columbus will visit Northland on Jun 25 at Woodward
Park Rec Center. This is a good way to meet city officials and fellow residents.
5. Officers were re-elected by acclamation.
6. Hoover Farms was suspended due to missed meetings.
7. Banquet is April 7 at Villa Milano, replacing the normal NCC meeting.
8. Independence Day Parade meetings are the 3rd Wednesday at the Y on Karl from 7-8 pm.
9. Note that the next round of work on 161 will likely demolish and remove the garden boxes along 161. If you want to
save any of that material, best do it after the bulbs bloom.

Hello Forest Park!
Spring has sprung - well, at least between the reoccurrences of winter. Oh,
Ohio. Officer Hicks has taken over for Officer Yandrich, who has moved on to
the mounted patrol unit, as our new police liaison, and he attended our March
meeting to introduce himself to the neighborhood. He's worked in various parts
of Columbus and plans on working in our area until he retires. We're lucky to
have him and are grateful for the years of service Officer Yandrich has provided
to our neighborhood! If you have any questions for him, please feel free to send
them to Daryl at security@fpcivic.org and he'll make sure to get you some answers. Officer Hicks can be
reached at dhicks@columbuspolice.org or 614-645-1418 but he prefers to have questions routed through a
single person. Please send all issues through Darly at the above email.
Now that the days are getting warmer and the night is getting shorter, there are a lot more
people out and about the neighborhood, so it's a good time for some reminders. When
parking in your driveway, ensure that your car is not overhanging the sidewalk. Not only
is it against Columbus City Code, blocking the sidewalk also puts pedestrians in danger
and makes travelling difficult for anyone with mobility issues. Also, kids are out playing
a little later so be mindful of your speed. 25MPH is the speed limit for almost the entire
neighborhood. Finally, don't leave your cars unlocked or your garage door open and
unattended. This invites people with ill intent to do
bad things, and we want everyone to know that
Forest Park is secure and we look out for each other.
If you're a paid member of the Forest Park Civic Association and are
planning on being out of town, don't forget to submit your vacation
request to Daryl Van Mercetta at security@fpcivic.org, online
at fpcivic.org, or mailing it to 2131 Honeytree Court, Columbus, OH
43229. As we start our membership drive, now is the perfect time to get
your membership in, only $30 for the entire household for the entire
year, and take full advantage of all the benefits it brings you.
As always, if you see something, say something and stay safe everyone.
